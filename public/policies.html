App Policies & Data Safety Disclosures

Specific operational commitments covering Android permissions, notification parsing rules, OTP protection, Family Workspace boundaries, and account deletion.

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 Google Play Compliance: Least-Privilege & Prominent Disclosure

1. Notification Auto-Tracking Policy (No SMS Permissions)

Zero SMS Inbox Access: RecapSpends does not declare or request READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, or call/contacts permissions. Your SMS inbox is never read.
  • Opt-In Prominent Disclosure: Before turning on expense alert auto-detection, RecapSpends presents an explicit in-app disclosure explaining what is read and what is ignored.
  • New Notifications Only: Android’s NotificationListenerService processes only newly posted notifications while the user is signed in and the feature is turned on. Past or existing notifications are never scanned.
  • Strict In-Memory OTP & Security Filtering: Any notification containing OTPs, one-time passwords, verification/auth/login/security codes, MPIN, CVV, "do not share", credits, refunds, failed transactions, or balance-only alerts is rejected immediately in memory—never saved locally, logged, or sent to any server.
  • Local Review Queue Until Approved: Qualifying payment/debit alerts are stored locally on the device in DataStore scoped to the active user ID. Signing out pauses new captures without erasing existing unapproved drafts. Drafts sync to Firestore only after you review and tap Approve.

3. Family Workspace Sharing & Removal Policy

  • Single Shared Workspace: Each account may belong to at most one Family Workspace at a time via invite code.
  • Granular Privacy Controls: Expenses flagged as Hide from Family Workspace and expenses assigned to categories marked Hidden from Family Workspace are strictly excluded from workspace sharing.
  • Complete Sharing Cutoff on Exit or Removal: Workspace creators may remove any member, and any member may leave at any time. Removing a member or leaving immediately revokes workspace membership and deletes every shared expense copy authored by that member from the workspace, leaving their personal expense history intact.

4. Acceptable Use Policy

Users agree not to:

  • Attempt to bypass Firebase App Check, Firestore security rules, or workspace invite controls.
  • Upload unlawful, fraudulent, or malicious payloads via the AI Bill Scanner or custom text fields.
  • Share invite codes publicly or add members to a Family Workspace without their consent.

5. Fresh Start & Account Deletion Policy

  • Start Fresh (Delete All Data): Wipes your personal expenses, vehicles, service logs, bill reminders, projects, and categories while removing your shared workspace copies. Your authentication login stays active so you can rebuild cleanly.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: Permanently erases all Firestore personal records, removes workspace memberships/shared copies, wipes your device-local Review drafts, and deletes your Firebase Authentication account.
