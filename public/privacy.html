Privacy Notice

Your Privacy in RecapSpends

How RecapSpends collects, stores, shares (only when you choose), and deletes your personal and household expense information.

Developer Contact: anbuselvampsna@gmail.com Effective Date: November 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect & Process

Data Type Where It Comes From How It Is Stored & Used
Account Identity Google Sign-In or Email/Password registration Firebase Authentication stores your UID, email address, display name, and optional Google profile photo URL to isolate your account.
Financial & Tracking Records Manual entry, approved Review drafts, AI bill scans, vehicles, bills, projects Stored in Cloud Firestore strictly under your authenticated UID (/users/{uid}/...) for syncing across your devices.
Unapproved Notification Drafts Opt-in NotificationListenerService (debit alerts only) Stored on-device only in Android Jetpack DataStore per UID until you approve, edit, or delete them. Never uploaded automatically.
Bill Images & PDFs User-selected Camera capture, Photo Picker, or PDF document Sent over encrypted TLS to Firebase AI Logic (Gemini) with App Check verification solely to extract structured expense lines when you request a scan.
Family Workspace Shares Opt-in Family Workspace membership Non-private expenses in visible categories are copied to /workspaces/{workspaceId}/expenses so authorized workspace members can view shared totals.

2. Data We Explicitly Do NOT Collect

  • No OTPs or Security Codes: One-time passwords, PINs, MPINs, CVVs, and verification alerts are filtered out immediately in memory and never logged, saved locally, or sent to any backend.
  • No SMS Inbox Reading: RecapSpends never accesses your SMS message database.
  • No Ad Tracking or Data Selling: RecapSpends displays no third-party advertisements and never sells or brokers your financial records to advertisers or third parties.

3. Security & Access Controls

  • Zero-Trust Firestore Rules: All personal subcollections require a verified Firebase Auth token matching the document owner’s UID. Cross-user reads or writes are rejected by Firestore security rules.
  • Workspace Membership Enforcement: Workspace documents and shared expenses are readable and queryable only by users actively listed in that workspace’s verified member roster.
  • Firebase App Check: Protects backend AI endpoints against unauthorized or tampered clients.

4. Your Choices & Controls

  • Pause Notification Reader Anytime: Disable the toggle in Review Alerts or revoke Notification Access in Android Settings at any time.
  • Hide Private Expenses & Categories: Mark individual expenses or whole categories as hidden from your Family Workspace so they stay strictly personal.
  • Leave or Kick Workspace Members: Leaving or removing a member immediately deletes all of their shared entries from the workspace and cuts off future sharing.
  • Export Your Data: Export your itemized & GST expense data as CSV anytime from Profile & Settings or Insights.

5. Data Retention & Complete Deletion

  • Sign-Out Behavior: Signing out clears your session and pauses notification capture while keeping any unapproved device-local Review drafts intact for your next sign-in.
  • Delete All Data (Start Fresh): Deletes all Firestore records in your account and shared workspace entries while keeping your login active.
  • Delete Account Permanently: Completely erases all Firestore personal records, workspace memberships/shared expenses, local device Review drafts for that UID, and your Firebase Auth login. You may also request deletion assistance by emailing anbuselvampsna@gmail.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

RecapSpends is intended for personal and household financial management by individuals aged 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction) and older, and does not knowingly collect personal data from young children.

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or security concerns, please contact the developer at anbuselvampsna@gmail.com.
