Quick Privacy & Control Highlights

  • Strict Account Isolation: Personal expenses, categories, vehicles, bills, and projects are stored under your private user path and protected by Firebase security rules.
  • No SMS Inbox Access: RecapSpends never requests READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS. Optional auto-tracking uses Android’s opt-in NotificationListenerService for new alerts only.
  • OTPs Ignored in Memory: One-time passwords, verification codes, MPIN alerts, credits, and balance-only alerts are discarded immediately in memory without storing or uploading them.
  • Device-Local Drafts: Unapproved transaction alerts stay on your device until you edit/map and approve them—and are preserved across sign-out.
  • Selective Family Sharing: Hide individual expenses or entire categories from your Family Workspace anytime. Leaving or removing a member deletes their shared workspace expenses completely.
